# Mission

## Purpose

Zach Hair Studio is a **services-led** platform. The salon experience —
booking hair styling and coloring appointments — is the heart of the product.
Selling hair-related products is a **supporting** offering that complements and
extends the service relationship, not the other way around.

## Who we serve

- **Clients** who want to discover styles/colors, book appointments (in person
  or online), and optionally buy the products their stylist recommends.
- **Staff** (stylists and front desk) who need a reliable dashboard to manage
  appointments, availability, clients, and product inventory.
- **Owner** who wants a reliable, attractive, modern site on a popular,
  maintainable stack.

## What we promise

1. **Booking is effortless.** Browsing services and reserving a slot is the
   primary, friction-free path.
2. **Products reinforce services.** Product catalog and purchases live
   alongside services and are framed as stylist-recommended extensions.
3. **Staff stay in control.** A clear dashboard gives staff fast access to the
   day's appointments and the tools to run the salon.
4. **Modern and attractive.** Fast, responsive, and polished on modern
   browsers and mobile.

## Priorities (in order)

1. Service discovery and appointment booking.
2. Staff dashboard for managing the schedule and clients.
3. Product catalog and purchasing.
4. Accounts, loyalty, and retention.

## Out of scope (for now)

- Marketplace / third-party sellers.
- Franchise / multi-location management.
- Native mobile apps beyond a responsive web experience.

## Success looks like

- A client can find a style/color and confirm an appointment in a few taps.
- Staff start their day from a single dashboard view.
- Products are bought as a natural add-on to a service, not a separate errand.
