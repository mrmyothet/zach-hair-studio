<objective>
Close the currently-ungated `ServicesController` write surface and deliver service image upload — the backend half of the MGMT-01 vertical slice. Owner-only gating (D-01) is applied at the action level so the public catalog GETs stay anonymous. Image upload (D-03) uses the framework-native `IFormFile` + static-file serving with server-generated filenames and a strict MIME/size allowlist. Soft-retire (D-02) is just `UpdateAsync` with `IsActive=false` — no new endpoint (exercised from the Plan 02 UI).

Purpose: Owner staff can create/edit/retire services and attach images from the dashboard without a deploy, and anyone POSTing services without an Owner token is rejected (closes a real shipped security gap).
Output: Gated write endpoints, a `POST {id}/image` upload endpoint, `ServiceImageUploadDto` + validator, `ServicesService.SetImageAsync`, static-file serving of `wwwroot/uploads/services/`, and integration tests proving the gate + upload behavior.
</objective>
