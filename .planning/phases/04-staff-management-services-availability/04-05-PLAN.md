<objective>
Wire the hard-block availability-conflict check into the availability save — the MGMT-03 vertical slice and the phase's sharpest correctness edge. On save, the server evaluates every Confirmed appointment for the target stylist against the full proposed final hours + time-off state; if any confirmed slot would fall outside the new hours or inside new time off, the save is refused (409) with an actionable inline conflict list, and nothing persists (D-09).

Purpose: Prevent availability edits from silently orphaning confirmed bookings — the phase goal's safety guarantee.
Output: `SalonTimeZone.ToSalonLocal`, `AvailabilityConflictDto`, the conflict scan in `AvailabilityService`, a `Result` conflict outcome + controller 409, the `ConflictList` panel wired into `/availability`, and the highest-priority conflict test suite.
</objective>
