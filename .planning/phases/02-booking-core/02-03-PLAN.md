## Phase Goal

**As a** client, **I want to** pick a service and book a real open slot with my chosen stylist, **so that** my appointment is confirmed and never double-booked.

> Vertical slice: the read side of booking. After this plan a client (via the API) can ask "what times are open for this service on this date, optionally with this stylist?" and get real, DST-correct answers derived from seeded working hours minus time off minus existing bookings. This is BOOK-01 and the time-math half of BOOK-05/BOOK-06.

<objective>
Build the open-slot query: a single DST-safe wall-clock→DateTimeOffset helper (SalonTimeZone), a SlotService that fetches the day's working hours / time off / booked cells server-side then generates 15-minute grid candidates in memory, and a thin GET /api/appointments/slots endpoint. Prove the grid math and DST edges with unit tests and the endpoint with an integration test.

Purpose: Deliver BOOK-01 and the timezone-correctness foundation (BOOK-05) that the write path (Plan 04) and the UI (Plan 05) both consume.
Output: SlotService + SalonTimeZone + OpenSlotDto, GET slots endpoint, and unit/integration tests including the Scalp Treatment cell-count and 2026 DST-boundary cases.
</objective>
