## Phase Goal

**As a** client, **I want to** add recommended products to a cart and check out as a guest with trustworthy, server-verified pricing and stock, **so that** I can complete a real purchase without creating an account.

> MVP slice 1 — guest cart API. After this plan, an anonymous client can create/update/review cart lines via REST under a session header. UI lands in Plan 02.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Primary noun: guest cart keyed by session (no ClientId on Cart).
Detected signal: "guest" / session-keyed ephemeral cart independent of accounts (Phase 7).
Decision: **promote** — Cart tables are session-scoped only; no ApplicationUser FK on Cart (CONTEXT Cart Architecture / D-02). Order.ClientId nullable is Plan 03 (D-06); do not invent a parallel "guest user" table here.
</assumption_delta_decision>
